About the Product

Two Filipino comfort foods, one smoky and one crispy.

Tinamai Usok

Tinamai is classic siomai made with tinapa (smoked fish). The smoky, savory flavor sets it apart from the usual siomai, so every bite tastes familiar and new at the same time.

Lumpken Crispy

Lumpken is lumpia filled with tender, well-seasoned chicken. It is wrapped in a thin spring roll wrapper and fried until golden, so you get a crunch and a juicy filling in each bite.

Privacy Notice

Tinamai at Lumpken is a food business in Las Piñas City, owned by five partners. We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, delivery address, notes, the items you ordered, and the total.
  • Payment details: your payment method and, if you pay with GCash, the reference number. We never see your GCash PIN or card details.
  • Account details (only if you sign up): your name, email, optional mobile number, and password. Our account provider stores the password securely, and we cannot read it.
  • Feedback: your star ratings and comments. Only your first name is shown next to a review.
  • On your device: your cart and a few settings are saved in your browser so the site works as expected. We do not use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To confirm your payment, prepare and deliver your order, and contact you about it.
  • To answer your questions and handle concerns.
  • To show ratings and reviews of our products.
  • To keep business records and protect the site from fraud and spam.

Who can see it

  • The partners of Tinamai at Lumpken, who handle orders and customer support.
  • The services that run this website: Supabase (database and accounts), Netlify (hosting and order alerts), Discord (a private team channel where new order alerts are posted, including your name, number, and address), and Google Fonts (page fonts, which may receive your IP address).
  • We do not sell your information. We share it with others only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to run the business, keep our records, and meet legal obligations. After that we delete them or remove your personal details.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to correct it, to object to its use, to ask us to erase or block it, to receive a copy of it, and to claim damages if it is misused. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
